M68EXORSET(D1) EXORset Users Guide
==================================

.. include:: M68EXORSET(D1).carousel.rst

.. Note:: 
   The binder spine images are labeled as being for the EXORset 30 Development System.
   They are for the EXORSET 35.
   Motorola had not yet updated all their released documents when they produced these manuals.

   Binder 1 contains the following documents:

   - `MSETBAS(S1)`
   - `MSETBAS(S1)`
   - `BASCNEWS(D2)`
   - `MSET30BAS(D1)`
   - `M68SETEDITORM(D1)`
   - `MSETLLD(D1)`
   - `MSETPLOT1(D1)`
   
   .. rubric:: Binder 2 contains the following documents:

   - `M68XDOS4(S1)`
   - `M68SETDS35(D1)`

   The Binders also include :

   - Disassembly diagrams
   - Program jumper settings
   - Pinouts
   - Schematics
   - Parts lists
   - CRT data and service manual
   - Floppy drive diagrams and servicing information
